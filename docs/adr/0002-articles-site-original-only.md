# Articles Are Site-Original Only — Institutional Statements Excluded

A politician's personal public profile must not mirror institutional outputs (government press releases, ministry communiqués, party statements). Republishing them blurs the line between personal voice and institutional voice, creates attribution ambiguity for readers and search engines, and risks drifting into an unofficial mirror of the institution. We therefore define the Article Content Item narrowly: only pieces authored by, or principally attributed to, Hamid himself — op-eds, essays, policy briefs. Formal institutional Statements stay on the institution's own website; if Hamid appears in a press interview, that is recorded as a Past Participation entry (interview-as-event) or a Gallery screenshot, not as an Article.
